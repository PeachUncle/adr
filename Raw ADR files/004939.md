# ADR-002: Domain Model Architecture for M&A Brokerage Platform

## Status

Accepted

## Date

July 25, 2025

## Context

The Advisory Platform requires comprehensive domain models to support M&A brokerage operations with strict regulatory compliance. We need to design domain entities that accurately represent the complex business relationships, financial data, regulatory requirements, and transaction workflows inherent in M&A transactions.

### Key Requirements

1. **Regulatory Compliance**: Support for state-specific business broker licensing, HSR filings, CFIUS reviews
2. **Financial Accuracy**: Precise financial modeling with audit trails and validation
3. **Transaction Lifecycle**: Complete M&A process from initial inquiry to post-closing
4. **Industry Specificity**: NAICS-based industry classification with regulatory considerations
5. **User Management**: Role-based permissions with licensing and compliance tracking
6. **Data Integrity**: Comprehensive validation and business rule enforcement

### Domain Complexity Factors

- Sub-$25M EBITDA focus requires specialized SME valuation approaches
- Multi-state operations require jurisdiction-specific compliance tracking
- Success-only fee model demands accurate deal outcome tracking
- Regulatory environment requires extensive audit trails and document management

## Decision

We will implement a **domain-driven design (DDD) approach** with the following core domain models:

### 1. Core Entities

**UserEntity**: Comprehensive user management with regulatory compliance

- Role-based permissions (Buyer, Seller, Advisor, Admin, Compliance Officer)
- Business licensing with jurisdiction-specific validation
- Compliance training tracking and certification management
- Security settings with MFA requirements for sensitive roles
- Audit trail integration for all user activities

**BusinessEntity**: Complete business profile for M&A transactions

- Industry classification using NAICS codes with regulatory mapping
- Comprehensive financial profiling with EBITDA normalization
- Valuation modeling with multiple-based reasonableness checks
- Asset categorization including real estate and intellectual property
- Regulatory risk assessment and compliance requirement mapping

**TransactionEntity**: Full M&A transaction lifecycle management

- Status-driven workflow with enforced progression rules
- Multi-party participant management with authorization tracking
- Deal structure modeling (asset vs stock purchase, payment terms)
- Regulatory requirement tracking (HSR, CFIUS, state approvals)
- Document lifecycle with execution tracking and compliance validation

### 2. Supporting Domain Models

**IndustryEntity**: Industry classification and regulatory framework

- NAICS hierarchy with SIC legacy support
- Industry-specific risk factors and regulatory considerations
- Valuation benchmarking and market conditions tracking
- M&A activity patterns and typical deal structures

**ComplianceEntity**: Regulatory compliance and tracking system

- Jurisdiction-specific regulatory requirements
- Compliance checklist generation and progress tracking
- Violation tracking with resolution workflows
- Compliance program management and performance metrics

### 3. Validation and Business Rules Engine

**ValidationEngine**: Comprehensive rule-based validation system

- Regulatory compliance validation with jurisdiction awareness
- Financial data consistency and reasonableness checks
- Transaction progression validation with required document tracking
- User authorization and licensing validation
- Configurable rule system for changing regulatory requirements

## Architectural Principles

### 1. Domain-Driven Design

- **Ubiquitous Language**: Consistent M&A terminology across all models
- **Bounded Contexts**: Clear separation between User, Business, Transaction, and Compliance domains
- **Aggregate Roots**: TransactionEntity serves as primary aggregate for deal management
- **Value Objects**: MonetaryAmount, Address, FinancialPeriod for consistency

### 2. Regulatory Compliance by Design

- **Audit Trails**: Every entity includes comprehensive audit logging
- **Data Classification**: Confidentiality levels and retention policies
- **Validation Rules**: Regulatory basis documented for all validation rules
- **Jurisdiction Awareness**: State and federal regulatory requirements modeled explicitly

### 3. Financial Data Precision

- **Decimal Precision**: String-based decimal amounts to avoid floating-point errors
- **Currency Consistency**: USD-focused with proper currency handling
- **GAAP Compliance**: Financial statement structuring follows accounting standards
- **Normalization Tracking**: EBITDA adjustments with supporting documentation

### 4. Transaction Integrity

- **State Machine**: Enforced transaction status progression with validation
- **Participant Authorization**: Role-based access with licensing verification
- **Document Lifecycle**: Complete document management with execution tracking
- **Milestone Tracking**: Progress monitoring with dependency management

## Implementation Decisions

### 1. Technology Choices

- **Zod Schemas**: Runtime validation with TypeScript integration
- **UUID Identifiers**: Secure, distributed-friendly entity identification
- **ISO DateTime**: Consistent timestamp handling across all entities
- **Result Pattern**: Error handling with audit trail preservation

### 2. Data Modeling Patterns

- **Composition over Inheritance**: Entity composition for flexibility
- **Immutable Value Objects**: MonetaryAmount, Address for consistency
- **Event Sourcing Ready**: Audit trails support future event sourcing
- **CQRS Friendly**: Separate read/write models where beneficial

### 3. Validation Strategy

- **Rule-Based Validation**: Configurable rules with regulatory basis
- **Multi-Level Validation**: Field, entity, and business rule validation
- **Context-Aware Rules**: Jurisdiction and role-specific validation
- **Severity Classification**: Info, Warning, Error, Compliance Violation

### 4. Cross-Package Dependencies

- **Core Independence**: @advisory/core has no dependencies on other advisory packages
- **Unidirectional Dependencies**: Other packages depend on core, not vice versa
- **Interface Segregation**: Clean interfaces for external package integration
- **Bounded Context Respect**: Each package maintains its domain boundaries

## Compliance Implications

### 1. Regulatory Adherence

- **State Licensing**: Business license validation with jurisdiction-specific rules
- **HSR Compliance**: Automatic threshold detection and filing requirement tracking
- **CFIUS Screening**: Foreign investment detection and review workflow
- **Corporate Transparency Act**: Beneficial ownership tracking and reporting

### 2. Audit and Reporting

- **Complete Audit Trails**: All entity changes logged with user attribution
- **Regulatory Reporting**: Data structures support required regulatory reports
- **Data Retention**: Configurable retention policies for compliance requirements
- **Access Controls**: Role-based access with audit logging for sensitive data

### 3. Data Protection

- **PII Handling**: Proper classification and protection of personal data
- **Financial Data Security**: Enhanced protection for financial information
- **Document Security**: Controlled access to transaction documents
- **Encryption Ready**: Data structures support field-level encryption

## Consequences

### Positive

- **Regulatory Compliance**: Comprehensive support for M&A regulatory requirements
- **Data Integrity**: Strong validation and business rule enforcement
- **Scalability**: Domain-driven design supports future feature expansion
- **Maintainability**: Clear separation of concerns with well-defined interfaces
- **Audit Readiness**: Complete audit trails for regulatory and business requirements

### Negative

- **Complexity**: Rich domain models require more development and testing effort
- **Performance**: Comprehensive validation may impact operation performance
- **Learning Curve**: Team needs understanding of M&A domain and regulatory requirements
- **Data Storage**: Rich models require more storage space and complex queries

### Neutral

- **Evolution Path**: Models can evolve as business requirements change
- **Technology Flexibility**: Domain-driven approach allows for technology changes
- **Integration Ready**: Well-defined interfaces support future integrations

## Migration and Implementation

### Phase 1: Core Entities (Current)

- Implement User, Business, Transaction, Industry, and Compliance entities
- Create comprehensive validation engine with regulatory rules
- Establish audit trail infrastructure

### Phase 2: Advanced Features

- Implement event sourcing for transaction history
- Add ML-ready data structures for matching algorithms
- Create reporting and analytics data views

### Phase 3: Integration

- Connect to external data providers (D&B, ZoomInfo)
- Integrate with document generation systems
- Add real-time compliance monitoring

## Monitoring and Metrics

### Model Quality Metrics

- **Validation Pass Rate**: Percentage of entities passing validation
- **Compliance Score**: Overall compliance assessment across entities
- **Rule Coverage**: Percentage of regulatory requirements covered by validation rules
- **Audit Trail Completeness**: Percentage of changes properly audited

### Business Impact Metrics

- **Transaction Progression**: Average time through each status
- **Compliance Violations**: Number and severity of compliance issues
- **Document Completion**: Percentage of required documents submitted
- **User Authorization**: Percentage of users with valid licenses/training
