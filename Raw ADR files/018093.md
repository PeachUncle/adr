# ADR-006: Seniority & Succession Matrix

## Status
Proposed

## Context
Engineering leaders need clear visibility into organizational depth and succession planning to make informed decisions about team structure, hiring priorities, and talent development. Currently, succession planning is often ad-hoc and lacks systematic analysis of seniority distribution across teams and geographies.

Key leadership challenges:
- **Succession blind spots**: Critical roles without obvious successors
- **Seniority imbalances**: Teams too junior/senior for their responsibilities  
- **Geographic risks**: Key expertise concentrated in single locations
- **Promotion pipeline visibility**: Understanding who's ready for advancement
- **Strategic workforce planning**: Identifying hiring vs. development needs

### Business Impact
Poor succession planning leads to:
- **Business continuity risks** when key people leave
- **Delivery slowdowns** from knowledge gaps
- **Retention issues** from blocked career progression
- **Hiring inefficiencies** from unclear skill/level gaps
- **Team performance** variations due to experience imbalances

## Decision
We will implement a **Seniority & Succession Planning Matrix** with interactive heatmap visualization showing:

1. **Organizational depth analysis** across teams and countries
2. **Succession readiness assessment** for critical roles
3. **Seniority distribution patterns** and imbalances
4. **Actionable insights** for workforce planning decisions

### Core Components

#### 1. Seniority Analysis Matrix
Visual heatmap showing distribution of experience levels:
- **Rows**: Teams or Product Domains
- **Columns**: Seniority Levels (Junior, Mid, Senior, Staff, Principal)
- **Cell Values**: Count of people at each intersection
- **Cell Colors**: Intensity based on concentration/balance

#### 2. Succession Readiness Assessment
Overlay showing preparedness for role transitions:
- **Succession Score**: 0-100% based on readiness factors
- **Time to Ready**: Estimated months until promotion-ready
- **Readiness Indicators**: Skills, experience, performance factors
- **Risk Flags**: Critical roles without successors

#### 3. Geographic Distribution View
Country/location-based analysis:
- **Location clustering**: Teams by primary geography
- **Expertise concentration**: Critical skills by location
- **Remote/distributed patterns**: Multi-location team analysis
- **Timezone impact assessment**: Collaboration risk factors

#### 4. Interactive Drill-Down
Progressive disclosure from overview to detail:
- **Matrix cell click**: List individuals in that team/level
- **Person profiles**: Detailed succession planning view
- **Team deep-dive**: Full team composition and readiness
- **Role-specific view**: Succession planning for specific positions

## Data Model & Calculations

### Seniority Level Classification
```ruby
# Based on organization_levels table
SENIORITY_MAPPING = {
  'E1' => 'Junior',      # 0-2 years experience
  'E2' => 'Junior',      # 0-2 years experience  
  'E3' => 'Mid',         # 2-5 years experience
  'E4' => 'Mid',         # 2-5 years experience
  'E5' => 'Senior',      # 5-8 years experience
  'E6' => 'Staff',       # 8-12 years experience
  'E7' => 'Principal',   # 12+ years experience
  'M1' => 'Manager',     # People management track
  'M2' => 'Sr Manager',  # People management track
  'M3' => 'Director'     # People management track
}
```

### Succession Readiness Score
Multi-factor assessment (0-100%):

**Performance Factor (40%)**
- Recent performance ratings
- Peer feedback and 360 reviews
- Goal achievement track record

**Skills Readiness (30%)**
- Required vs. current skill alignment
- Technical competency for next level
- Leadership/management capabilities

**Experience Factor (20%)**
- Time in current role
- Breadth of project experience
- Cross-functional exposure

**Aspiration Factor (10%)**
- Expressed interest in advancement
- Career development plan participation
- Training/certification completion

### Risk Indicators
- **Single Point of Failure**: Only one person at senior level
- **Succession Gap**: No clear successor for key role
- **Experience Cliff**: Large gap between senior and mid levels
- **Geographic Concentration**: Critical skills in one location
- **Promotion Bottleneck**: Multiple ready candidates, limited positions

## Technical Architecture

### Data Sources
```ruby
# Core organizational data
- users (with current roles/levels)
- organization_role_assignments
- organization_level_assignments  
- organization_team_assignments
- organization_countries (for geography)

# Performance and readiness data
- performance_reviews (if available)
- skill_assessments
- career_development_plans
- training_completions

# Derived metrics
- tenure_in_role calculations
- promotion_eligibility scoring
- succession_readiness_cache
```

### Calculation Pipeline
```
1. Daily Succession Analysis Job
   ↓
2. Seniority Distribution Calculator
   ↓
3. Succession Readiness Evaluator
   ↓  
4. Risk Pattern Detector
   ↓
5. Matrix Data Aggregator
   ↓
6. Cache Update & Invalidation
```

### Performance Strategy
- **Pre-calculated matrices**: Daily batch processing
- **Incremental updates**: Only recalculate changed entities
- **Efficient queries**: Proper indexing on role/level/team assignments
- **Caching layers**: Redis for heatmap data, browser cache for visualizations
- **Lazy loading**: Load detailed views only when requested

## Risk Mitigation

### Privacy & Sensitivity
- **Career discussions**: Information might influence sensitive conversations
- **Succession politics**: Visible succession plans may create office politics
- **Performance implications**: Readiness scores shouldn't substitute for performance reviews

**Mitigations:**
- Clear access controls by management level
- Focus on organizational patterns vs. individual assessments
- Regular calibration with HR and management teams

### Data Quality Risks
- **Stale role assignments**: Outdated team/level information
- **Subjective readiness factors**: Inconsistent succession assessments
- **Missing skills data**: Incomplete skill assessments affecting calculations

**Mitigations:**
- Automated data validation and freshness checks
- Clear indicators of data quality and last update times
- Fallback calculations when complete data unavailable

### Organizational Change Impact
- **Rapid growth**: Matrix may become outdated quickly in scaling orgs
- **Reorganizations**: Team structure changes invalidate cached calculations
- **Role evolution**: New roles/levels not reflected in seniority mapping

**Mitigations:**
- Incremental update system for organizational changes
- Configurable seniority mappings and thresholds
- Change detection and automatic recalculation triggers

## Technical Considerations

### Scalability
- **Large organizations**: Efficient handling of 500+ person organizations
- **Matrix size**: Performance with 50+ teams and multiple geographies
- **Real-time updates**: Incremental calculation vs. full rebuilds

### Browser Performance
- **Heatmap rendering**: Efficient DOM manipulation for large matrices
- **Interactive features**: Smooth filtering and drill-down performance
- **Memory usage**: Optimized data structures for client-side calculations

### Data Architecture
- **Denormalized views**: Pre-calculated succession matrices
- **Event-driven updates**: Invalidation on organizational changes
- **Archive strategy**: Historical succession data for trend analysis

## Decision Outcome
Implement an interactive Seniority & Succession Matrix that provides visual, actionable insights into organizational depth and succession planning. The system will help engineering leaders make informed decisions about team structure, hiring priorities, and talent development through clear visualization of seniority distribution and succession readiness across teams and geographies.

The implementation prioritizes practical succession planning over comprehensive people analytics, ensuring managers can quickly identify risks and opportunities in their organizational structure.
